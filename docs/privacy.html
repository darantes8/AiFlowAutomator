Privacy Policy
Privacy by design

Your automation rules stay in your browser.

AI Flow Automator is designed to automate repetitive interactions on supported AI chat websites. The extension detects user-defined text in rendered responses and can automatically submit the response configured by the user, without sending your rules or conversation content to the developer's servers.

Local settings Automation rules and preferences are stored in Chrome storage.
No tracking We do not use browsing or conversation data for ads or profiling.
No data sales Your extension data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

⚙️ Data stored by the extension

AI Flow Automator stores only the information needed to provide its automation features and preserve the user's configuration between browser sessions.

  • user-defined expected text rules;
  • user-defined response text;
  • rule search mode and related rule settings;
  • response delay and automation preferences;
  • enabled or disabled state;
  • interface preferences required by the extension.
These settings are stored using Google Chrome storage APIs and are not sent to servers operated by the developer.

👁️ How page content is used

To perform automation, the extension needs to inspect rendered text on supported AI chat pages and interact with the message input when a configured rule matches.

This access is used only while providing the extension's automation functionality. Conversation content is processed in the browser and is not intentionally collected, stored as conversation history, or transmitted to the developer's servers.

🔐 Data we do not collect

AI Flow Automator does not intentionally collect or store:

  • your name, email address, or other personal identification information;
  • passwords, credentials, authentication tokens, or account secrets;
  • financial or payment information;
  • health information;
  • precise location;
  • browsing history for analytics or profiling;
  • AI conversation content on developer-controlled servers;
  • data for advertising, behavioral tracking, or user profiling.

🌐 Host permissions

Host permissions are required so AI Flow Automator can run on supported AI chat websites. These permissions allow the extension to read rendered response text, detect configured rules, and interact with the page's message input when automation is enabled.

Host access is used only for the extension's user-requested functionality and not for advertising, unrelated monitoring, or building profiles of browsing activity.

↗ Transmission and sharing

AI Flow Automator does not sell, rent, or share extension settings or AI conversation content with third parties.

The extension does not use your stored rules or page content for advertising, marketing, profiling, or purposes unrelated to its automation features.

🧩 Remote code

AI Flow Automator does not rely on remotely hosted JavaScript or WebAssembly to provide its functionality. Executable extension code is packaged with the extension distributed through the Chrome Web Store.

🗑️ Deleting extension data

Users can change or remove automation rules through the extension's settings. Stored extension data can also be removed by clearing the extension's site or storage data through Chrome, or by uninstalling the extension.

⚠️ Third-party websites

AI Flow Automator operates on third-party AI chat websites. Those websites have their own privacy policies and data practices, which are separate from this policy. This Privacy Policy describes only the behavior of the AI Flow Automator extension.

🛠️ Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if AI Flow Automator adds new functionality or changes how extension data is handled. When that happens, this page will be updated to reflect the current behavior of the extension.

✉️ Contact

For questions about privacy or the operation of AI Flow Automator, use the support contact listed on the extension's official Chrome Web Store page or in the project's official repository.
